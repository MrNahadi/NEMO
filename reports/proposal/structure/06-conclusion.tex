\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}

The proposal defines a focused engineering study of a parameterized marine equipment bracket. Its purpose is to determine whether systematic sampling and derivative-free search can reduce bracket mass while retaining strength and stiffness. Analytical screening will guide the search, while Fusion FEA will provide the intended compliance check.

After the bracket method has been validated, future studies may apply it to a gusseted lifting padeye and the internal structure of a stabilizer fin. Those extensions will require component-specific load paths, failure checks, mesh studies, and, for the stabilizer, a defensible hydrodynamic pressure model.
