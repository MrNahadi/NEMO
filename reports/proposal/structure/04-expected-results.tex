\chapter{Expected Results}

The proposed work is expected to produce a parameterized bracket model, a documented baseline, sampled and optimized bracket candidates, and a Fusion validation record. Results will compare mass, maximum von Mises stress, minimum factor of safety, and maximum displacement. No predetermined percentage reduction will be assumed. Success will require identification of a bracket lighter than the baseline that satisfies the Fusion factor-of-safety and deflection limits.

The study is also expected to identify where analytical beam and plate approximations differ from three-dimensional FEA. These differences will guide appropriate safety margins and future refinement. Padeye and stabilizer results will not form part of the proposed results.
