\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

This proposal presents the planned engineering optimization of an Aluminium 6061-T6 marine equipment mounting bracket carrying a 50~kg auxiliary unit. The study will define a six-variable bracket geometry, apply a 1471.5~N design load, and use analytical screening, Latin-hypercube sampling, bounded Nelder--Mead search, and Fusion 360 validation to seek a lower-mass design subject to a minimum factor of safety of 2.5 and maximum deflection of 0.5~mm. The bracket is the sole case study within the proposal. Extension of the method to marine lifting padeyes and stabilizer structures is identified as future work after the bracket workflow has been validated.
