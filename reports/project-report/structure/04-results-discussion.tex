\section{Bracket Results}
The baseline used a 150 by 100 by 8~mm baseplate, 40~mm rib height, 6~mm rib thickness, and 5~mm fillet radius. Table~\ref{tab:bracket-results} summarizes the analytical campaign.

The best sample reduced mass by 39.8\% but remained lightly utilized. The baseline-start search required 132 evaluations and approached the deflection constraint. The sample-start search required 134 evaluations and produced the lightest screened design: 100.09~mm length, 80.00~mm width, 4.01~mm base thickness, 33.64~mm rib height, 3.00~mm rib thickness, and 4.11~mm fillet radius.

The predicted reduction was
\begin{equation}\frac{0.418125-0.112593}{0.418125}\times100=73.1\%.\end{equation}
Most dimensions approached their lower bounds. This reveals both the opportunity and risk of optimization: the algorithm exploited the simplified model and limits provided. Minimum plate gauge, bolt bearing, weldability, corrosion allowance, fatigue, vibration, and fabrication tolerance must be added before accepting such thin members.

\section{Engineering Interpretation}
Mass reduced with plate plan dimensions and thickness, while retained rib height preserved section depth and bending stiffness. The two searches converged to different rib-height and fillet combinations, confirming sensitivity to the starting point. Both final designs operated near the analytical FOS limit, so conservative backup candidates should accompany the lightest design during FEA.

\section{Padeye and Stabilizer Outcomes}
For the padeye, the project completed the load case, material model, nine-variable screening model, CAD generator, boundary definitions, and validation package. Critical regions are the pin-hole net section, bearing surface, lug root, gusset roots, and welded interfaces.

For the stabilizer, the project completed the fixed-envelope internal-structure model, pressure bands, analytical screening, CAD generator, and validation package. Critical regions are the root, spar connections, skin-spar intersections, and trailing edge. These extensions demonstrate method portability but not service-ready designs.

\section{Validation Status}
CAD generation and export were achieved, but recorded Fusion maximum stress, FOS, and displacement values were absent from the validation checklists. All numerical structural results in Table~\ref{tab:bracket-results} are therefore analytical predictions. No claim of classification approval, FEA compliance, or fabrication readiness is made.
