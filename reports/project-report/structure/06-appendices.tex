\chapter{Appendices}

\section{Required Validation Record}
Each final FEA record should include material assignment, mesh sizes and element count, boundary-condition screenshots, reaction-force balance, maximum von Mises stress, minimum FOS, maximum displacement, convergence evidence, mass, pass/fail status, and engineering comments.

\section{Reproducibility}
The reported bracket campaign comprised 30 sampled evaluations, 132 evaluations in the baseline-start search, and 134 evaluations in the sample-start search. Source CSV and JSON records are retained in the formal run directories.
