\section{Stabilizer Pressure-Band Distribution}
\label{app:pressure-bands}

The prescribed stabilizer resultant was distributed over four spanwise bands
using the relative weights

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{w}=[1.00,\ 0.93,\ 0.75,\ 0.40].
\end{equation}

The normalized fractions and corresponding forces are given in
Table~\ref{tab:app-pressure-bands}. Pressure on each band is obtained by
dividing its assigned force by the corresponding CAD surface area.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Prescribed stabilizer pressure-band forces}
\label{tab:app-pressure-bands}
\begin{tabular}{crr}
\toprule
\textbf{Band} & \textbf{Normalized fraction} & \textbf{Force (N)}\\
\midrule
1 & 0.324675 & 3833.8\\
2 & 0.301948 & 3565.4\\
3 & 0.243506 & 2875.1\\
4 & 0.129870 & 1533.8\\
\midrule
Total & 1.000000 & 11\,808.0\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

These loads are declared structural inputs, not CFD predictions. A later
hydrodynamic study must establish the operational pressure field and relevant
positive, negative, and torsional load cases.
