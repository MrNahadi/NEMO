\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

This project investigated the constrained mass optimization of marine structural components using parameterized geometry and first-order structural models. The primary case was an Aluminium 6061-T6 equipment bracket carrying a 50~kg auxiliary unit under a 1471.5~N design load. A 30-point Latin-hypercube study and two bounded Nelder--Mead searches were completed. The baseline analytical mass was 0.4181~kg; the lightest screened candidate had a mass of 0.1126~kg, factor of safety 2.60, and deflection 0.361~mm, giving a 73.1\% analytical mass reduction. The method was extended to an S275 lifting padeye and a fixed-envelope aluminium stabilizer fin through engineering definitions, analytical models, CAD generators, and validation packages. Fusion stress results were not recorded in the available validation tables; consequently, reported stress and deflection values are screening predictions requiring mesh-converged FEA and physical validation before fabrication.
